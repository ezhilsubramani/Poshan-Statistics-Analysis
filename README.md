# 📊 Poshan Statistics Analysis: Monitoring Nutrition and Anganwadi Service Performance in India

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Poshan Statistics Dataset** to evaluate the performance of Anganwadi Centres (AWCs), beneficiary coverage, nutrition service delivery, and healthcare initiatives across India.

The analysis focuses on identifying trends, comparing state-wise performance, and generating business insights to support better decision-making in the Poshan Abhiyaan program.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Malnutrition among children, pregnant women, and lactating mothers remains a significant public health challenge in India.

The Government of India operates Anganwadi Centres (AWCs) under the Poshan Abhiyaan scheme to provide:

- Nutritional support
- Growth monitoring
- Health services
- Supplementary nutrition
- Early childhood care

This project analyzes Poshan Statistics data to understand program performance and identify areas that require improvement.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze beneficiary coverage across Indian states.
- Evaluate Anganwadi Centre performance.
- Study Take Home Ration (THR) and Hot Cooked Meal (HCM) distribution.
- Assess Anganwadi infrastructure availability.
- Compare Aadhaar verification with Health ID creation.
- Generate interactive dashboards for decision-making.
- Provide business insights and recommendations.

# 📊 Key Findings

- Uttar Pradesh has the highest beneficiary coverage.
- Rural Anganwadi Centres significantly outnumber Urban Centres.
- Aadhaar verification is much higher than Health ID creation.
- THR distribution is substantially higher than HCM distribution.
- States with larger beneficiary populations generally report higher service utilization.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

- Rural regions remain the primary service delivery channel.
- Significant opportunity exists to improve Health ID registration.
- Hot Cooked Meal service continuity requires improvement.
- Dashboard-based monitoring helps identify high-performing and low-performing states.
